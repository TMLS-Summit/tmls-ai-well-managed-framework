# Section III. Example industry use cases

## 1. Supply Chain Forecasting

A global consumer goods company deployed an AI-driven demand forecasting model integrated with its ERP and point-of-sale systems. By ingesting historical sales, weather patterns, and promotional calendars, the model reduced forecast error by 22% and cut excess inventory by 18%. Business ownership was assigned to the Supply Chain VP, with governance checkpoints ensuring data freshness and model retraining every quarter. The initiative was prioritized using a value-vs-feasibility matrix, with data readiness confirmed prior to MVP launch.

## 2. Fraud Detection

A mid-sized financial institution implemented a real-time transaction scoring model to flag potentially fraudulent activity. Trained on three years of labelled transaction data, the model achieved a 34% reduction in false positives while increasing fraud catch rates. The Chief Risk Officer served as executive sponsor, ensuring alignment with regulatory requirements and AI ethics guidelines. Model drift monitoring and a defined retraining cadence were embedded into the governance framework from inception, with measurable outcomes tracked against pre-defined KPIs.

## 3. Credit Scoring

A retail lending division adopted an ML-based credit scoring model to supplement its traditional bureau-based process, incorporating alternative data such as utility payments and rental history. The initiative expanded credit access to underserved segments while maintaining portfolio quality. Bias audits were conducted pre-deployment as part of the AI governance review, and the model owner in the Credit Risk team ensured explainability requirements were met for regulatory compliance. Approval rates and default rates were tracked as core business impact metrics.

## 4. Transportation

A regional logistics operator deployed an AI-powered route optimization engine that ingested live traffic feeds, delivery windows, and vehicle telematics. The system reduced average delivery time by 14% and fuel costs by 11% within the first six months. Strategic alignment was established by mapping the initiative to corporate cost reduction and sustainability objectives. A cross-functional team spanning Operations and IT co-owned the solution, with adoption metrics tracked through driver app engagement rates and on-time delivery performance.

## 5. Manufacturing

A discrete manufacturer deployed a predictive maintenance model on its assembly line, using sensor data from connected equipment to forecast failures before they occurred. Unplanned downtime dropped by 27% in the pilot plant within nine months. The Plant Operations Director held business ownership, with success criteria defined at project initiation. Data readiness was assessed upfront, identifying gaps in sensor coverage that were remediated before model training. Lessons learned were fed into an iteration loop to extend the model to two additional facilities.
